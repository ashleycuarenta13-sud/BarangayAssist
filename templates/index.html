ABOUT BARANGAYASSIST

A simpler way to report community concerns.

BarangayAssist gives residents an online place to submit concerns and check their progress without having to visit the barangay hall just to make an initial report.

01

Submit a Concern

Describe the issue you want the barangay to know about using the concern submission form.

03

Check Progress

Use the available status information to see the progress of a concern you submitted.
