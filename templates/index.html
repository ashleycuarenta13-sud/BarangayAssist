What It Does

Our Services

Everything needed to file, classify, and follow up on a community concern.

SVC-01 / INTAKE

Concern Submission

A short, guided form for reporting any issue - infrastructure, peace, and order, health, or sanitation.

SVC-02 / ROUTE

Smart classification

NLP reads the concern text and tags it by category and urgency before it reaches staff.

SVC-03 / TRACK

Status Tracking

Residents follow their concern's progress from received to in-progress to resolved.

SVC-04 / CLOSE

Feedback & Closure

Residents confirm resolution and leave feedback once the concerned office responds.
